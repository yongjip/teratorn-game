Support

Need help, hit a bug, or have feedback on Teratorn? We’d love to hear from you.

📧 Email yongjip@gmail.com — please include your device and OS version if you’re reporting a problem, so we can help faster.

Quick answers

  • How do I play? Take resource tokens, buy Project Cards to build your infrastructure and earn influence, and race to be first to dominate Mars. Tap a card to buy it (or reserve it if you can’t afford it yet).
  • Do I need an account? No. Single-player and online multiplayer both work without any sign-up — multiplayer uses an anonymous connection.
  • Is it online-only? No — you can play a full match against the AI completely offline. Online multiplayer is optional.

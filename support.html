Support

Need help, hit a bug, or have feedback on Teratorn? We’d love to hear from you.

📧 Email yongjip@gmail.com — please include your device and OS version if you’re reporting a problem, so we can help faster.

Quick answers

  • How do I play? Take resource tokens, buy Project Cards to build your infrastructure and earn influence, and race to be first to dominate Mars. Tap a card to buy it (or reserve it if you can’t afford it yet).
  • Do I need an account? Yes. Initial setup uses a free Unity Player Account and requires internet. Use the same sign-in method to recover your progress on another device.
  • Is it online-only? After initial setup, Solo works offline. Online matches, account recovery, friends and cloud synchronization require internet.
  • Inviting a friend? Send an in-game invitation or share the room link, QR code or room code. Background iOS invitations need notification permission and an internet connection; delivery can be affected by system notification settings.
  • Account access? Manage your sign-in at Unity Player Accounts. Deleting that shared account does not automatically delete Teratorn game data.
  • Privacy? See our Privacy Policy.

Collect resources. Buy cards. Build permanent discounts. Read your rivals and compete for points.

Plan your next move

Choose resource tokens, buy a card from the shared market or reserve one for later. Purchased cards reduce future costs. Build your discounts while scoring points. When a player reaches the target score, the final round begins; the highest score wins.

Your next match on Mars

  • Play Solo against three AI difficulty levels or compete online with up to four players total.
  • Invite friends to a private room by link, QR code or room code.
  • 90 Project Cards, 10 Legends and your own space avatar.
  • Portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad. No ads.

A free account and internet connection are required for initial setup. After setup, Solo is available offline; online play requires an internet connection.

App Store

Collect resource tokens, complete Project Cards and build permanent discounts. Compete for Influence and Legends in Solo or online matches for up to four players total. Make each turn count on the way to Splendorra.

What it plays like

  • Play Solo against AI, join Quick Match, or create a private room with friends.
  • 90 Project Cards and 10 Legends.
  • Easy to learn, deep to optimize — a tidy tableau-builder.
  • No ads. Initial setup requires a free account and internet; Solo is available offline afterward.

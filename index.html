Teratorn

Race to industrialize Mars.

Coming soon to iOS & Android

Teratorn is a clean, brain-tickling engine-builder card game set on a B-movie Mars frontier. You're a greedy rocket tycoon: take resource tokens, fund Project Cards, and turn cheap launchpads into orbital monopolies and terraforming megaprojects. First to dominate wins.

What it plays like

  • Quick, satisfying matches (~20 min) against a real AI opponent — or online with friends.
  • 54 project cards + 10 Mars charters, hand-drawn industry art.
  • Easy to learn, deep to optimize — a tidy tableau-builder.
  • No ads, no accounts, no nonsense.

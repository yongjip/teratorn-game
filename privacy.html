Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Teratorn uses account and game data to save your progress and let you play with friends. We do not use the game data for advertising or sell it.

Accounts and saved progress

Account setup uses Unity Player Accounts. Unity collects an email address and account identifier to operate that shared account and handles the sign-in credentials; Teratorn receives a game authentication identifier and linked account identifier. Your shared Unity Player Account is separate from your Teratorn game account.

We store your chosen display name, avatar, game progress and statistics on your device and in Unity Cloud Save to support backup and recovery. Account setup and synchronization require an internet connection. After setup, Solo can be played offline; changes can synchronize when you reconnect.

Friends and multiplayer

Unity Gaming Services processes friend relationships, incoming and outgoing requests, blocks, invitation records, room membership, connection status and match traffic. Your display name, avatar and relevant game information are visible to the players you interact with. Temporary limits and invitation records protect these features against misuse.

Optional invitation notifications

If you permit notifications, a device notification token is associated with your game account so that a friend's invitation can reach your device. Apple Push Notification service delivers iOS notifications. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in your device settings.

Service providers and support

Unity provides authentication, cloud storage and multiplayer services. See Unity's Privacy Policy for its own services and operational processing. Requests to online services also expose network information such as an IP address to Unity; this can be processed for service operation and security and is distinct from GPS or precise device location. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

What the game does not request

The game does not request your address book or precise location and does not use an advertising identifier. It has no advertising SDK. Game analytics delivery is disabled in the current build; installation-local first-match state is used on the device. Camera permission, where used for scanning an invitation QR code, is for that action.

Delete your game account

You can initiate game-account deletion from the account and privacy controls in your player profile. The request covers game authentication, saved progress, friend relationships and requests, outgoing blocks, invitation and notification associations, and matching local recovery data. Blocks made by another player remain under that player's control.

Deletion requires an internet connection and involves several services. If the app reports an incomplete request or asks you to retry, deletion has not been confirmed complete. Contact support below if the request cannot finish or you can no longer sign in. We may need to verify ownership before acting on a request. Temporary processing records and access restrictions may be used to finish deletion and prevent interrupted requests from restoring deleted data. Unity's handling of its own operational records is described in its privacy policy.

Deleting Teratorn does not delete your shared Unity Player Account or accounts in other games. Manage that shared account through Unity Player Accounts deletion.

Children

Teratorn is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the app, contact us so we can investigate and address the request. The App Store age rating describes content and does not replace account eligibility requirements.

Privacy questions

Contact yongjip@gmail.com for questions or help with an interrupted deletion. We may need to verify account ownership. If your installed version does not show the account controls, contact support for help.

We will update this page when the game's data practices change. Requests concerning Unity's separately operated account or its infrastructure are handled under Unity's policy.
